— From the Editor
The most radical thing a wardrobe can do, in a year when everything is for sale, is to remain quietly the same.

For our fourteenth issue, we asked seven women across four continents to dress, for a single weekend, only in the clothes they have already owned for more than five years. What came back wasn't a fashion story. It was an inheritance.

Yuki Ogawa photographed each of them by natural light, indoors, without retouching. The garments sat on bodies that had lived in them. There is no styling credit; there is, instead, a list of repairs.
